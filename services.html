Things to Do

Glide down real snow high in the Maluti Mountains. Lessons and rental gear are usually available. The season is short, so check dates before you travel.

Take a guided 4x4 tour up the famous gravel road. Stop at the top to warm up and look out over the mountains.

Walk forest trails and gorges, or ride a horse through the valleys. Start early and wear layers.

Meet a local guide and learn how families built their homes into the sandstone. Ask before you take photos of people.

Bring a picnic, watch the birds and wait for the sunset. It is a gentle day out.
